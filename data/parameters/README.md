# Parameters

Numbers measured from real observations (action durations, success rates, etc.) -- what we
measured, not what we assumed or what the rules assert. Empty until empirical estimation begins
(M5, per `docs/roadmap.md`). Each future entry carries `program: v5rc | vexu` (never `both`),
`transfer_class: direct | program_specific | uncertain`, `source_observations`, `n`, `method`,
`distribution`, `fitted_on: train | full`, `date`.

Never mix parameters into `data/rules/` or `data/assumptions/` -- see `CLAUDE.md`,
"Hard constraints."
